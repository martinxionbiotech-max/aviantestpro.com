    Team & Expertise¶

    Background¶

    The accuracy and reliability of avian genetic testing depend fundamentally on the expertise of the personnel performing the analysis. SENO employs 25 full-time professionals, with 20 dedicated to laboratory operations, quality control, and research & development. The team spans multiple disciplines — molecular genetics, ornithology, veterinary science, bioinformatics, and quality management — providing comprehensive coverage of the testing workflow from sample receipt through result interpretation.

    Frequently Asked Questions¶

    Q: How are new laboratory technicians trained? A: New technicians undergo a 3-month supervised training program covering SOPs, equipment operation, contamination control, and QC procedures. Competency is assessed through blind sample testing before independent work is authorized.

    Q: Does SENO employ veterinarians? A: SENO's team includes molecular biologists and laboratory scientists. For clinical interpretation of test results — particularly disease testing — SENO recommends consultation with a licensed avian veterinarian.

    Q: What languages does the support team speak? A: Full support in Chinese (Mandarin) and English. Additional language support for Japanese, Korean, and Arabic is available through team members and translation services.
