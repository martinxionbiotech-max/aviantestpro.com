About the Laboratory¶

Zhangjiakou SENO Testing Center Co., Ltd. is a professional avian genetic testing laboratory established in Zhangjiakou, Hebei Province, China. As a National High-Tech Enterprise, SENO specializes in DNA-based sex determination, disease diagnostics, and genetic analysis for birds. The laboratory serves customers in 20+ countries across Asia, Europe, the Middle East, and the Americas.

Background¶

The laboratory was founded in 2015 in response to growing demand for accurate, affordable avian molecular diagnostics. China's position as both a major market for pet birds (particularly parrots and canaries) and a hub for pigeon racing created a unique need for specialized avian testing services that general veterinary laboratories were not equipped to provide.

SENO's founders identified that most existing avian testing services were either: - General veterinary labs without species-specific expertise - Academic research groups not oriented toward commercial turnaround times - Overseas providers with prohibitive shipping costs for Asian clients

By establishing a dedicated avian molecular diagnostics facility in Zhangjiakou, SENO addressed this gap — combining the expertise of species-specialist scientists with the efficiency of a commercial testing operation.

Technical Capabilities¶

SENO's service offerings span the full range of molecular avian diagnostics:

DNA Gender Testing¶

The core service uses PCR amplification of the CHD-W and CHD-Z genes, present on the avian sex chromosomes. The CHD gene's intron size polymorphism between the Z and W chromosomes allows gender discrimination in 250+ validated species. The laboratory uses two primer sets (P2/P8 and 2550F/2718R) to handle species where fragment co-migration occurs.

Disease Diagnostics¶

Quantitative PCR (qPCR) assays detect viral, bacterial, and fungal pathogens with high sensitivity (typically 10 copies/reaction limit of detection). The disease testing menu spans: - Individual pathogen assays: PBFD, APV, AIV (H5/H7/H9 subtypes) - Multi-pathogen panels: 11-pathogen pigeon panel (viruses, bacteria, fungi) - Custom panels: Available for research and collection screening

Racing Pigeon Genetics¶

Specialized testing for Columba livia racing pigeons includes: - Performance gene markers (DRD4, LDHA, CK, MB) - DNA fingerprinting for individual identification - Parentage verification for pedigree validation

Custom Reagent Development¶

SENO's R&D team develops and validates proprietary primer/probe sets, PCR master mixes, and sample collection reagents optimized for specific species and sample types.

Quality Assurance¶

All testing is performed under ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016 certified quality management systems. Key quality practices include:

  • Pre- and post-PCR physical separation to prevent amplicon contamination
  • Duplicates on 20% of samples for internal consistency verification
  • Positive and negative controls on every PCR plate
  • Internal amplification controls (synthetic RNA/DNA) on qPCR assays
  • Proficiency testing through semi-annual inter-laboratory comparisons

Frequently Asked Questions¶

Q: How long has SENO been in operation? A: SENO was established in 2015, with over a decade of continuous operation in avian molecular diagnostics.

Q: What countries does SENO serve? A: SENO serves 20+ countries across Asia (Japan, South Korea, Thailand, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Philippines), Europe (UK, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, France, Italy, Spain), the Middle East (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar), and North/South America.

Q: Can SENO handle custom testing requests? A: Yes. The R&D team can develop and validate custom PCR and qPCR assays for specific pathogens, species identification, or research applications. Contact SENO with your requirements.

Q: What is the typical turnaround time? A: Standard DNA sexing results are reported within 3–5 business days of sample receipt. Disease testing takes 5–7 business days. Express service (1–2 business days) is available upon request.
