Racing Pigeon Genetics¶

Advanced genetic analysis for racing pigeon breeding, performance optimization, and pedigree verification. SENO's genetics division, based in Zhangjiakou, China, develops and validates molecular markers specifically for racing pigeon (Columba livia domestica) populations. Our testing panel covers four established performance-associated genes plus high-resolution DNA fingerprinting and parentage verification systems.

Why Genetic Testing for Racing Pigeons?¶

Genetic testing provides pigeon fanciers and breeders with objective data to inform breeding decisions. By understanding the genetic makeup of individual birds, breeders can:

BenefitDescriptionImpact on Performance
Identify performance potentialDRD4, LDHA, CK, and MB markers correlate with racing traits5–15% improvement in race outcome prediction
Verify parentageDNA-based confirmation of pedigree recordsPrevents fraud in high-value bird trade
Prevent theftPermanent genetic identity for each birdDefinitive ownership proof
Manage genetic diversityInbreeding coefficient calculationsReduces loss of heterozygosity
Optimize pairingsMarker-assisted selection for complementary traitsMore consistent offspring quality
Document breeding linesLong-term genetic record keepingTraceable lineage across generations

Performance Gene Panel Overview¶

DRD4 (Dopamine Receptor D4)¶

AttributeDetail
Full NameDopamine Receptor D4
FunctionNeurotransmission regulation; linked to novelty-seeking, motivation, and activity levels
Polymorphism14-bp indel in exon 3 (VNTR)
Associated PhenotypeHigher activity levels, endurance, racing motivation
Allele DesignationLong (L) / Short (S) — LL genotype associated with better long-distance performance
Validation SourceSENO internal study (n = 120 racing pigeons, 2024)
Heritability EstimateModerate (0.35–0.45)

LDHA (Lactate Dehydrogenase A)¶

AttributeDetail
Full NameLactate Dehydrogenase A
FunctionAnaerobic glycolysis; converts pyruvate to lactate; muscle energy metabolism
PolymorphismSNP in promoter region (c.-97T>C)
Associated PhenotypeLactate clearance rate, muscle endurance
CC GenotypeHigher LDHA expression, faster lactate clearance, better sprint performance
TT GenotypeLower LDHA expression, slower lactate clearance, reduced sprint capacity
Validation SourceSENO internal study (n = 120 racing pigeons, 2024)
Heritability EstimateModerate (0.30–0.40)

CK (Creatine Kinase)¶

AttributeDetail
Full NameCreatine Kinase — Muscle-type
FunctionATP regeneration in muscle cells; phosphocreatine energy shuttle
PolymorphismIntronic SNP (g.IVS2-46A>G)
Associated PhenotypeMuscle energy metabolism, recovery rate after exertion
GG GenotypeHigher CK activity, faster ATP regeneration, improved recovery
AA GenotypeLower CK activity, slower recovery
Validation SourceSENO internal study (n = 120 racing pigeons, 2024)
Heritability EstimateModerate (0.30–0.42)

MB (Myoglobin)¶

AttributeDetail
Full NameMyoglobin
FunctionOxygen storage and transport in cardiac and skeletal muscle
PolymorphismSNP in coding region (c.47A>G; Lys15Arg)
Associated PhenotypeAerobic endurance, oxygen buffering capacity
GG GenotypeHigher myoglobin affinity or expression; better endurance performance
AA GenotypeLower oxygen buffering; better in shorter distances
Validation SourceSENO internal study (n = 120 racing pigeons, 2024)
Heritability EstimateModerate (0.28–0.38)

Sample Collection Instructions¶

Feathers¶

StepInstruction
1Select 5–7 chest or wing feathers
2Pluck firmly (do not cut) to include the calamus (quill base)
3Place in a clean paper envelope
4Label envelope with bird's ring number and date
5Store at room temperature — stable for years

Blood (FTA Card)¶

StepInstruction
1Use a sterile lancet to prick the medial metatarsal vein
2Touch the blood drop to the FTA card circle (fill completely)
3Allow to air dry for 1 hour
4Label card with bird's ring number and date
5Store in a sealed plastic bag at room temperature
