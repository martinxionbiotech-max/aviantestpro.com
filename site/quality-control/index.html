SENO operates a multi-tier quality control framework that governs every stage of the avian genetic testing workflow — from sample reception through DNA extraction, PCR amplification, result interpretation, and final report issuance. This framework is aligned with our ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016 certified quality management systems and follows CLSI EP guidelines for assay validation.
