SENO Avian Genetic Test — Technical Documentation Center¶

Welcome to the official SENO Testing Center technical documentation knowledge base. This resource provides structured technical references, laboratory protocols, quality documentation, and regulatory compliance guidance for avian genetic testing services.

Company: Zhangjiakou SENO Testing Center Co., Ltd. Main Website: aviantestpro.comLocation: Zhangjiakou, Hebei, China Certifications: ISO 9001:2015 · ISO 13485:2016 · National High-Tech Enterprise Service Scope: DNA sexing, disease diagnostics, and genetic analysis for 250+ bird species across 20+ countries

Our Mission¶

This documentation exists to:

  • Support bird breeders, veterinarians, and research institutions with standardized, verifiable technical references for avian genetic testing services
  • Provide method-specific laboratory protocols for CHD-W/CHD-Z PCR sexing, BFDV qPCR, APV detection, and microsatellite genotyping
  • Establish quality benchmarks for avian diagnostic testing including validation parameters, proficiency testing, and accuracy monitoring
  • Guide sample collection and international shipping with species-specific protocols, packaging requirements, and customs documentation
  • Document racing pigeon genetics — performance markers, DNA fingerprinting, and parentage verification for breeding programs

Documentation Categories¶

🧬 DNA Gender Testing¶

Detailed technical references for avian sex determination using CHD-W/CHD-Z gene marker analysis: - PCR methodology — P2/P8 and 2550F/2718R primer sets, hot-start Taq, 35-cycle protocol - Species compatibility — Validated for 250+ species across Psittacidae, Columbidae, Fringillidae, Anatidae, Struthionidae, and more - Fragment analysis — Capillary electrophoresis (preferred) vs. agarose gel electrophoresis - Interpretation — Single band (male ZZ), two bands (female ZW), co-migration variants

Sections: Gender Testing | PCR Method | Parrot Sexing | Pigeon Sexing | Species Compatibility

🔬 Avian Disease Testing¶

Molecular diagnostic assays for avian pathogens with published analytical validation: - PBFD (BFDV) qPCR — TaqMan probe, ORF1 target, LOD 10 copies/reaction, 95.9% sensitivity, 98.0% specificity - Avian Polyomavirus (APV) — PCR detection from feather, blood, or swab samples - Pigeon Disease Panel — 11-pathogen multiplex qPCR including PiCV, PiHV, PiADV, Chlamydia psittaci, Salmonella, Candida, and PPMV-1 - Avian Influenza Surveillance — H5/H7/H9 subtyping RT-PCR for monitoring programs

Sections: Disease Testing | PBFD | Pigeon Panel | AIV Surveillance

🕊️ Racing Pigeon Genetics¶

Advanced genetic analysis for racing pigeon breeding and identification: - Performance Gene Markers — DRD4 (homing behavior), LDHA (energy metabolism), CK (sprint potential), MB (oxygen transport) - DNA Fingerprinting — 12-locus microsatellite panel, >99.99% discrimination, <1 in 2.5×10¹⁵ match probability - Parentage Verification — Exclusion-based parent confirmation with duo/trio analysis - Population Database — 2,500+ pigeon profiles for allele frequency reference

Sections: Pigeon Genetics | Performance Genes | DNA Fingerprinting | Parentage Verification

📋 Sample Collection & Shipping¶

Standardized protocols for proper sample handling across species: - Feather Collection — Freshly plucked follicle preservation (3–7 feathers per bird) - Blood Sampling — FTA card storage, EDTA tube, blood spot on filter paper - Swab Collection — Oral and cloacal swab for disease detection, stabilization buffer - International Shipping — IATA biological substance category B, CITES documentation, customs clearance - Storage & Stability — Room temperature (feather), refrigeration (blood), stabilization buffer (swab)

Sections: Sample Guide | Feather Protocol | Shipping Guide | Quick Reference

🧪 Testing Methods & Technology¶

Technical information about SENO's molecular testing platform: - Conventional PCR — End-point amplification, gel and capillary detection - qPCR (Real-Time) — TaqMan probe, SYBR Green, multiplex fluorescent channels (FAM, VIC, ROX, Cy5) - Capillary Electrophoresis — Automated fragment sizing, 4-dye multiplex capability - Self-Developed Reagents — In-house primer design, probe synthesis, master mix optimization - Validation Framework — CLSI EP guidelines for LOD, precision, accuracy, interference testing

Sections: Testing Methods | PCR Technology | qPCR | Capillary Electrophoresis | Reagent Development

✅ Quality Control & SOPs¶

Laboratory quality framework and standard operating procedures: - DNA Extraction SOP — Alkaline lysis, silica column, magnetic bead methods - CHD PCR Protocol — Step-by-step SOP with primer preparation, thermocycling program, interpretation - PBFD qPCR Validation — Full analytical validation report per CLSI EP guidelines - PCR Troubleshooting Guide — 50+ diagnostic entries for amplification failure, contamination, artifacts - Proficiency Testing — Internal and external programs, blind sample exchange - Accuracy Monitoring — Ongoing validation, lot-to-lot reagent qualification, annual review
