International Shipping Regulations for Avian Testing Samples¶

SENO's laboratory in Zhangjiakou, Hebei, China, receives samples from over 20 countries worldwide. Understanding and complying with international shipping regulations is essential for ensuring timely delivery and avoiding customs delays. This guide covers the full regulatory framework, sample classification, documentation requirements, and best practices for sending avian biological samples to SENO for diagnostic testing.

Step 2: Packing Procedure¶

  1. Place sample in primary container (tube or envelope) and seal securely
  2. Place primary container inside secondary container with absorbent material
  3. Seal secondary container
  4. Place secondary container inside outer packaging with cushioning material
  5. Attach address label, customs documents, and UN3373 label (if applicable)
  6. Photograph the final package for record-keeping

Step 3: Shipping¶

  1. Schedule courier pickup or drop off at service center
  2. Declare correctly: "Feather samples for DNA testing — no commercial value"
  3. Obtain tracking number and provide to SENO
  4. Send customs documents to SENO in advance if requested
  5. Avoid weekend delivery: Schedule for Monday–Thursday arrival
  6. Monitor tracking and notify SENO of any delays

Prohibited Items¶

The following cannot be shipped to SENO:

  • Samples not labeled correctly (no ID, no species)
  • Samples in glass containers (risk of breakage)
  • Samples leaking or with visible contamination
  • Samples from CITES Appendix I species without permits
  • Live animals
  • Samples containing known Category A pathogens

Emergency Contact¶

If a shipment encounters an issue during customs clearance, contact SENO immediately:

  • Email: Provided upon client registration
  • Tracking number: Required for all inquiries
  • Response time: Within 2 hours during business hours (GMT+8)
