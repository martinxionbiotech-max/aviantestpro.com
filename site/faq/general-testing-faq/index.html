    General Testing FAQ¶

    How accurate is avian DNA sexing?¶

    SENO's avian DNA sexing achieves approximately 99% accuracy using validated PCR-based methods with internal quality controls and sample retesting. This high accuracy rate is maintained through:

    Quality MeasureContribution to Accuracy
    Validated CHD gene primersTargets conserved region across bird species
    Positive and negative controls per runDetects assay failure or contamination
    Internal amplification control per sampleConfirms DNA quality and absence of PCR inhibition
    ~20% random retestingCatches any rare technical errors
    Second technician reviewIndependent verification of all results
    External proficiency testingIndependent validation of laboratory performance

    Accuracy by Species Category¶

    Species GroupNumber of Validated SpeciesDocumented Accuracy
    Psittacines (parrots)200+~99.2%
    Columbiformes (pigeons, doves)15+~99.5%
    Passerines (canaries, finches)30+~98.5%
    Galliformes (chickens, quail)10+~99.0%
    Anseriformes (ducks, geese)10+~98.0%

    Note: Most reported discrepancies come from species with unusual CHD gene band patterns (e.g., certain parrot species where the CHD-Z and CHD-W bands are very similar in size). SENO validates each new species before offering sexing services and flags known challenging species in reports.

    What is the difference between PCR and qPCR?¶

    FeatureConventional PCRqPCR (Real-Time PCR)
    Detection methodEnd-point (after reaction)Real-time (during reaction)
    ReadoutGel electrophoresis (bands)Fluorescence curves (Ct values)
    SensitivityModerate (detects ≥ 10² copies)High (detects ≤ 10 copies)
    QuantificationSemi-quantitativeFully quantitative (with standard curve)
    Turnaround time2-3 hours per run1-2 hours per run
    Contamination riskHigher (post-PCR handling)Lower (closed-tube system)
    Data richnessBinary (present/absent)Kinetic (amplification curve)
    Cost per sampleLowerSlightly higher
    SENO applicationsDNA sexing, parentage, fingerprintingDisease detection, pathogen quantification

    How long does testing take?¶

    Most tests are completed within 1–2 working days after sample arrival at the laboratory.

    What is the total turnaround time including shipping?¶

    Total turnaround (from shipment to receiving results) is approximately 7–10 business days, depending on shipping and customs processing. The breakdown is:

    PhaseDurationNotes
    International shipping3–5 business daysDHL, FedEx, or UPS
    Customs clearance1–3 business daysVaries by country of origin
    Sample registration and processing0.5 business dayUpon arrival
    DNA extraction0.5 business dayStandard protocol
    PCR/qPCR analysis0.5–1 business dayDependent on test type
    Result interpretation and review0.5 business daySecond technician review
    Report generation and delivery0.5 business dayPDF via email
    Total (estimated)7–10 business daysFrom shipment to results

    Rush service: Contact SENO to inquire about expedited processing for urgent cases.
