Shipping FAQ¶

Do you accept international samples?¶

Yes. SENO regularly receives samples from breeders, pet shops, veterinary partners, and distributors worldwide. Our laboratory in Zhangjiakou, Hebei, China, processes samples from over 20 countries across multiple continents.

New country? Contact SENO to confirm that shipping from your country is feasible. We provide specific guidance for each country of origin.

How do I send samples to your laboratory?¶

Contact us before shipping for current instructions. Feather samples in paper envelopes sent via international courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS) is the standard method.

Pre-Shipment Checklist¶

1Register your accountContact SENO to set up client account
2Prepare samplesCollect and package according to sample type guidelines
3Complete submission formInclude bird IDs, species, test type required
4Package for shippingFollow packaging guidelines for your sample type
5Schedule courierDHL, FedEx, or UPS tracked express service

Is international shipping safe for feather samples?¶

Yes, based on long-term experience. Feather samples are low-risk when properly packaged. Sending multiple samples together reduces per-sample cost.

How long does shipping take?¶

International shipping typically takes 3–5 business days, plus 1–3 days for customs processing at the destination.

What happens if my package is held by customs?¶

If a shipment encounters a customs hold, the following procedure is activated:

StepActionResponsible
1SENO identifies delay (tracking monitoring)SENO Logistics
2Contact client to inform of customs holdSENO Logistics
3Send supporting customs documents to brokerSENO Logistics
4Contact courier for assistanceSENO Logistics + Client
5Customs releases samplesCustoms authority
6Samples delivered to SENOCourier
7Samples processed (may have minor delay)SENO Laboratory

Important: Always share your tracking number with SENO upon shipment. This allows us to proactively monitor for customs issues and intervene before samples are returned to sender.

Can I use a local courier in China?¶

No. International samples must be shipped via DHL, FedEx, or UPS (tracked express services). Local Chinese couriers (e.g., SF Express domestic) cannot process international customs clearance.
