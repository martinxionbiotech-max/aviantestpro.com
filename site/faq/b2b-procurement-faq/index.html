B2B Procurement FAQ — Avian DNA Testing Services¶

Contact us by email for current ordering procedures. We provide sample submission forms and shipping instructions.

2. Sample Submission¶

How should I send samples from multiple birds?¶

Each bird must be sampled separately. Recommended packaging: 1. Each bird's feathers in a separate paper envelope 2. Envelopes grouped in a single padded mailer 3. Submission form outside the envelopes (to prevent contamination) 4. Label each envelope with the bird's ID

What information must accompany each sample?¶

Required FieldExample
Bird ID / NameAG-047
SpeciesAfrican Grey Parrot
Test RequiredDNA Sexing
Sample TypeFeather
Collection Date2026-04-15

Can I pool samples to save cost?¶

Pooling (mixing samples from multiple birds) is not recommended for sexing — mixed DNA produces ambiguous results. For disease screening, pooled testing (3–5 birds per pool) is available as a cost-saving surveillance option.

Do you accept international samples?¶

Yes. SENO processes samples from 20+ countries. We provide full customs documentation support including commercial invoices and customs letters. Shipping guidance is available upon request.

4. Quality & Guarantees¶

What accuracy rate do you guarantee?¶

SENO guarantees ≥ 99% accuracy for DNA sexing tests based on our internal validation. Discrepant results are investigated at no charge.

What if I disagree with a result?¶

SENO offers free retesting on any result the client disputes: 1. Notify us within 30 days of report 2. Provide a fresh sample (if sample condition is in question) 3. Free retest with alternate primer set 4. If discrepancy is confirmed: full refund + free retest of remaining batch

Do you offer refunds?¶

ScenarioRefund Policy
Lab error (confirmed)Full refund + free retest
Sample quality failureFree retest with fresh sample
Client cancels before processing100% refund
Result is unexpected but accurateNo refund (confirmed by QC review)
