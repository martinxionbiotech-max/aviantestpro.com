# Sample Collection & Shipping Guide


Welcome to the SENO Avian Genetic Test sample collection and shipping guide. This document serves as the central hub for all collection protocols, shipping procedures, and quality assurance standards used by the SENO avian genetics laboratory.

## About SENO's Laboratory

SENO operates a dedicated avian genetics laboratory that accepts biological samples from birds shipped to our facility from over **20 countries** worldwide. Our laboratory performs DNA sexing, genetic disease screening, DNA fingerprinting, and pathogen detection using validated molecular workflows optimized for avian samples.

## Accepted Sample Types

SENO accepts three primary sample types for genetic analysis. Each sample type has specific advantages, collection requirements, and stability profiles.

| Sample Type | Primary Uses | Shipping Requirements | DNA Yield |
|:------------|:-------------|:---------------------|:----------|
| Freshly plucked feathers | DNA sexing, genetic screening | Room temperature, paper envelope | Medium |
| Blood (FTA card, EDTA tube, filter paper) | DNA fingerprinting, disease testing | Room temp (FTA card) or cold pack (EDTA) | High |
| Swab (oral/cloacal with stabilization buffer) | Disease testing, pathogen detection | Cold pack recommended | Medium–Low |

## Sample Comparison Overview

Choosing the right sample type for your test is critical. The table below compares key attributes across all accepted formats.

| Attribute | Feather | Blood (FTA card) | Blood (EDTA) | Swab (stabilized) |
|:----------|:--------|:-----------------|:-------------|:------------------|
| Invasiveness | Minimal | Moderate | Moderate | Low |
| Difficulty | Easy | Moderate | Advanced | Easy |
| DNA yield | Medium | High | Highest | Low–Medium |
| Room temp stability | Up to 90 days | Years | 7 days | 14 days |
| Best for sexing | ✓ Yes | ✓ Yes | ✓ Yes | Limited |
| Best for disease | Limited | ✓ Yes | ✓ Yes | ✓ Yes |
| Requires special supplies | No | Yes (FTA card) | Yes (tube, needle) | Yes (stabilization kit) |

## Quality Assurance Standards

SENO maintains strict quality controls throughout the sample collection and analysis pipeline. All submitted samples are subject to the following quality checks:

- **Sample integrity inspection** — Visual inspection upon receipt for contamination, desiccation, or mold
- **DNA quantification** — Spectrophotometric assessment of DNA yield and purity (A₂₆₀/A₂₈₀ ratio)
- **PCR inhibition screening** — Internal control amplification to detect inhibitory substances
- **Species confirmation** — Genetic marker verification for target species
- **Chain-of-custody documentation** — All samples tracked from receipt to result reporting

## Tests Offered by Sample Type

Different genetic tests at SENO require different sample formats. The recommended sample type varies by test category.

| Test Category | Preferred Sample | Acceptable Alternatives | Notes |
|:--------------|:-----------------|:------------------------|:------|
| DNA Sexing | Freshly plucked feathers | Blood (FTA card), swab | Feathers provide > 98% success rate |
| Disease Screening (individual) | Swab (oral + cloacal) in stabilization buffer | Blood (EDTA or FTA card) | Swab preferred for respiratory/enteric pathogens |
| DNA Fingerprinting / Parentage | Blood (EDTA or FTA card) | Feathers, blood filter paper | Blood provides highest resolution for microsatellite analysis |
| Species Identification | Feathers or blood (FTA card) | Swab | Any sample with intact DNA works |
| Pathogen Strain Typing | Swab (stabilized) | Fresh tissue | Requires high-quality RNA/DNA |
| Post-mortem / Necropsy | Fresh tissue or swab | Blood | Tissue must be collected within 4 hours of death |

## Common Sample Rejection Reasons

SENO tracks sample quality upon receipt. The most common reasons for sample rejection are listed below.

| Rejection Reason | Affected Sample Types | Prevention |
|:-----------------|:----------------------|:-----------|
| Feather mold from plastic bag storage | Feathers | Use paper envelopes only |
| Insufficient DNA (shed feathers) | Feathers | Pluck fresh; verify follicle bulb |
| Hemolyzed blood | Blood (EDTA) | Gentle collection; avoid vigorous mixing |
| Buffer leakage from swab tube | Swabs | Tighten caps; tape cap-to-tube junction |
| Cross-contamination between birds | All types | Change gloves between birds |
| Improper labeling or missing ID | All types | Label immediately after collection |
| Delayed shipment (> 4 weeks at room temp) | Feathers, swabs | Ship within 2 weeks of collection |
| Frozen FTA card (damages paper matrix) | Blood (FTA card) | Store at room temperature |
| Missing submission form | All types | Check contents before sealing package |

## Before You Submit

1. **Confirm the test type** with your SENO representative to select the correct sample format
2. **Review the collection protocol** specific to your chosen sample type
3. **Prepare the shipping package** according to the international shipping guidelines
4. **Complete the submission form** with accurate bird identification and owner details
5. **Ship early in the week** (Monday–Wednesday) to avoid weekend delays in transit
6. **Notify SENO** of the shipment by emailing the tracking number and courier details
