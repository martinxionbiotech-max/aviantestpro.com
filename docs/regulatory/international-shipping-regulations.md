# International Shipping Regulations for Avian Testing Samples


SENO's laboratory in Zhangjiakou, Hebei, China, receives samples from over 20 countries worldwide. Understanding and complying with international shipping regulations is essential for ensuring timely delivery and avoiding customs delays. This guide covers the full regulatory framework, sample classification, documentation requirements, and best practices for sending avian biological samples to SENO for diagnostic testing.

## Regulatory Framework

International shipment of avian biological samples involves compliance with multiple regulatory frameworks:

| Regulation | Authority | Scope | Applicability at SENO |
|:-----------|:----------|:------|:---------------------|
| IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations | International Air Transport Association | Air transport of biological substances | All shipped samples classified as Category B (UN3373) |
| CITES | Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species | Transport of material from protected species | Required for Appendix I/II species |
| National Import Regulations | Customs per destination country | Import of biological diagnostic samples | China customs clearance |
| Biosafety Regulations | National health authorities | Pathogen-containing sample transport | Applicable for disease testing samples |
| China Customs Biosecurity | General Administration of Customs China | Import of animal-derived materials | All incoming biological samples |

## Sample Classification for Shipping

| Sample Type | UN Classification | Proper Shipping Name | Packing Instruction | Special Requirements |
|:------------|:-----------------:|:---------------------|:--------------------|:--------------------|
| Feathers (no visible contamination) | Exempt (not regulated) | "Laboratory testing samples" | Standard padded envelope | Paper envelope only — no plastic |
| Swabs in stabilization solution | UN3373 | Biological Substance, Category B | PI 650 | Triple packaging system |
| Blood (EDTA, < 10 mL) | UN3373 | Biological Substance, Category B | PI 650 | Leak-proof primary container |
| Blood (FTA card) | Exempt (dried blood) | "Dried blood spots on FTA card" | Standard envelope | Allow to dry completely before packing |
| Tissue samples | UN3373 | Biological Substance, Category B | PI 650 | In stabilization solution |
| Eggshell membrane | Exempt (not regulated) | "Eggshell membrane samples" | Standard padded envelope | Air-dried, in paper envelope |

## Packaging Requirements for UN3373 (Category B)

| Layer | Requirement | Example | Specification |
|:------|:------------|:--------|:-------------|
| Primary container | Leak-proof, watertight | Screw-cap tube with rubber seal | ≤ 1 L liquid capacity |
| Secondary container | Leak-proof, watertight | Sealed plastic bag or rigid container | Absorbent material between primary and secondary |
| Outer packaging | Rigid, durable | Corrugated cardboard box, rigid plastic courier envelope | ≥ 100 mm smallest dimension |
| Labeling | UN3379 diamond mark | Category B label | 50×50 mm minimum |
| Documentation | Shipper's declaration (if required) | IATA DGR checklist | Completed by trained shipper |

## CITES Compliance

| CITES Appendix | Species Examples at SENO | Documentation Required | Owner Responsibility |
|:---------------|:-------------------------|:----------------------|:--------------------|
| Appendix I | Blue-throated Macaw, Spix's Macaw | Export permit + Import permit | Client arranges both permits |
| Appendix II | Most parrot species (Ara, Amazona, Cacatua, Psittacus) | Export permit (or certificate) | Client arranges export permit |
| Appendix III | Country-specific listed species | Certificate of origin | Client confirms status |
| Non-CITES | Pigeons (Columba livia), Budgerigars, Canaries, Finches | No CITES documentation required | No CITES action needed |

## Step-by-Step International Shipping Protocol

### Step 1: Pre-Shipment Preparation

| Action | Details | Timeline |
|:-------|:--------|:--------|
| Contact SENO | Notify us before shipping from any country | 1-2 business days before shipping |
| Verify species | Check CITES status of your bird species | Before packing |
| Prepare samples | Collect and package according to sample type | Same day as shipping |
| Prepare documents | Complete customs documentation | Before courier pickup |
| Choose carrier | DHL, FedEx, or UPS (tracked express service) | Book in advance |

### Step 2: Packing Procedure

1. Place sample in primary container (tube or envelope) and seal securely
2. Place primary container inside secondary container with absorbent material
3. Seal secondary container
4. Place secondary container inside outer packaging with cushioning material
5. Attach address label, customs documents, and UN3373 label (if applicable)
6. Photograph the final package for record-keeping

### Step 3: Shipping

1. Schedule courier pickup or drop off at service center
2. **Declare correctly:** "Feather samples for DNA testing — no commercial value"
3. Obtain tracking number and provide to SENO
4. Send customs documents to SENO in advance if requested
5. **Avoid weekend delivery:** Schedule for Monday–Thursday arrival
6. Monitor tracking and notify SENO of any delays

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Cause | Solution | Prevention |
|:------|:------|:---------|:-----------|
| Customs hold | Incomplete or inaccurate declaration | Contact SENO — we send supporting documents | Complete all customs paperwork accurately |
| Shipment returned | Country-specific import restrictions | Check with SENO before shipping for known restrictions | Pre-shipment verification |
| Clearance delay | Missing commercial invoice or value declaration | Always include commercial invoice with declared nominal value | Prepare all documents in advance |
| Sample seized | CITES documentation missing or incorrect | Verify species CITES status before shipping | CITES paperwork completed before shipment |
| Package damaged | Insufficient packaging | Ensure triple-layer packaging with cushioning | Follow IATA packaging guidelines |
| Delivery to wrong address | Incorrect labeling | Verify SENO address before shipping | Confirm address with SENO |
| Delayed processing at customs | Holiday or inspection backlog | Notify SENO of tracking number for monitoring | Avoid shipping before public holidays |

## Prohibited Items

The following **cannot** be shipped to SENO:

- Samples not labeled correctly (no ID, no species)
- Samples in glass containers (risk of breakage)
- Samples leaking or with visible contamination
- Samples from CITES Appendix I species without permits
- Live animals
- Samples containing known Category A pathogens

## Emergency Contact

If a shipment encounters an issue during customs clearance, contact SENO immediately:

- **Email:** Provided upon client registration
- **Tracking number:** Required for all inquiries
- **Response time:** Within 2 hours during business hours (GMT+8)
