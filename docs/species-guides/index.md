# Species Guides


SENO's species-specific testing guides provide tailored recommendations for different bird groups, considering species-specific variations in CHD intron size, sampling techniques, and disease susceptibility.

## Background

Not all birds are the same — at least not from a molecular testing perspective. The CHD gene intron varies in size across species, affecting primer set selection and fragment analysis method. Sample types yield different DNA amounts depending on bird size. Disease susceptibility varies dramatically between taxonomic groups — PBFD is a concern in parrots but essentially absent in pigeons, while pigeons carry PiCV and PPMV-1 that are rare in parrots. These differences make species-specific guidance essential for optimal testing outcomes.

SENO's species guides consolidate decades of laboratory experience into practical recommendations organized by taxonomic group.

### Primer Selection by Group

| Group | Standard Primer | Backup Primer | Rationale |
|:------|:---------------:|:-------------:|:----------|
| Psittacines (large) | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | Reliable separation in most large parrots |
| Psittacines (small) | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | Co-migration more common in small species |
| Columbiformes | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | P2/P8 gives 25–30 bp separation — gold standard |
| Passerines | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | Generally reliable; finch co-migration occasionally reported |
| Galliformes | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | Well-separated in chickens, quail |
| Anseriformes | P2/P8 | 2550F/2718R | Some duck species may need 2550F/2718R |

## General Guidelines by Group

| Group | Minimum Feathers | Preferred Sample | PCR Primer Set | Recommended Disease Screening |
|:------|:----------------:|:-----------------|:---------------|:------------------------------|
| Parrots (large) | 3–5 | Chest feather | P2/P8 | PBFD, APV |
| Parrots (small) | 5–7 | Chest feather | P2/P8 | PBFD, APV |
| Pigeons | 3–5 | Chest feather | P2/P8 (well resolved) | 11-pathogen panel |
| Canaries/Finches | 5–7 | Chest feather | P2/P8 (may need 2550F/2718R) | AIV, APV |
| Poultry | 3–5 | Blood or feather | P2/P8 | AIV (H5/H7/H9) |
| Waterfowl | 3–5 | Blood preferred | P2/P8 (2550F/2718R for some) | AIV (H5/H7/H9) |

## Sample Type Recommendations by Group

| Group | Best Sample | Acceptable Sample | Avoid |
|:------|:------------|:------------------|:------|
| Large parrots (>300 g) | 3–5 chest feathers | Blood (0.3–0.5 mL EDTA), buccal swab | Shed feathers |
| Small parrots (<100 g) | 5–7 chest feathers | Blood (0.1–0.2 mL EDTA) | Buccal swab (too high failure rate) |
| Pigeons (300–500 g) | 3–5 chest feathers | Blood (0.1–0.2 mL), FTA card | Eggshell membrane |
| Canaries (10–25 g) | 5–7 chest feathers | Blood (0.05 mL max), FTA card | Buccal swab (very high failure rate) |
| Finches (10–20 g) | 5–7 chest feathers | Blood (0.05 mL FTA card) | Any invasive method (fragile birds) |

## Disease Susceptibility by Group

| Pathogen | Parrots | Pigeons | Passerines | Poultry |
|:---------|:-------:|:-------:|:----------:|:-------:|
| PBFD (BFDV) | ❤️ High | ❌ None | ❌ None | ❌ None |
| APV | ❤️ High | ❌ None | ○ Rare | ❌ None |
| PiCV | ❌ None | ❤️ High | ❌ None | ❌ None |
| PPMV-1 | ❌ None | ❤️ High | ❌ None | ○ Moderate |
| AIV H5/H7/H9 | ○ Moderate | ○ Moderate | ○ Moderate | ❤️ High |

> ❤️ High = Common / clinically significant. ○ Moderate = Occurs but not the primary concern. ❌ None = Not a known natural host.

### Conservation Breeding Programs

Captive breeding programs for endangered species rely on accurate sex identification for:

- **Sex-ratio management:** Ensuring equal representation of males and females in breeding colonies
- **Pair formation:** Confirming male-female pairs for genetic management
- **Juvenile sexing:** Identifying sex of hatchlings before banding and release
- **Genetic diversity management:** Avoiding over-representation of one genetic lineage

SENO supports conservation programs with discounted testing rates for registered conservation breeding facilities. Contact SENO for program details.

### International Trade & Import/Export

Many countries require health certification and/or species-specific testing for imported birds:

| Region | Typical Requirements |
|:-------|:---------------------|
| European Union | AIV (H5/H7) PCR + 30-day quarantine; CITES for listed species |
| United States (USDA APHIS) | AIV + NDV testing; 30-day quarantine |
| China | AIV + NDV testing; import permit required |
| UAE / Middle East | AIV PCR for pigeons; health certificate from origin country |
| Japan | AIV PCR + 30–60 day quarantine for parrots |

SENO's ISO-certified testing supports documentation for international movement.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why do small parrots need more feathers than large parrots?**
A: The feather follicle size scales with the bird. A macaw feather follicle might yield 50–100 ng of genomic DNA, while a budgerigar follicle might yield only 5–15 ng. Since PCR requires a minimum template of ~20 ng per reaction, more small feathers are needed to compensate.

**Q: Can I apply parrot recommendations to my lovebird?**
A: Lovebirds are technically psittacines (parrots), so the small parrot recommendations apply: use 5–7 feathers and consider capillary electrophoresis for fragment analysis as CHD-W and CHD-Z are close in size.

**Q: My species is not listed in any of the three guides. Where should I look?**
A: Check the [Species Compatibility Matrix](../gender-testing/species-compatibility.md) for a complete list. If your species is not listed there, contact SENO for feasibility assessment.
