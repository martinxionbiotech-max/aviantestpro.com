# Intellectual Property


## Background

Intellectual property (IP) protection is a cornerstone of SENO's competitive advantage in avian molecular diagnostics. The laboratory's patent portfolio reflects sustained investment in research and development — spanning novel detection methods, optimized reagent formulations, specialized laboratory equipment, sample collection devices, and software systems for data management. As of 2026, SENO holds 2 invention patents, 18 utility model patents, 3 software copyrights, and 3 IC layout designs.

## Awards

- **Hebei Provincial Science & Technology Award (2023)** — Recognized for innovation in avian molecular diagnostics, specifically for developing species-specific PCR primer panels and stabilized sample collection reagents for international shipping.

### Software Systems

SENO's internally developed software includes:

1. **SENO LIMS (Laboratory Information Management System)** — Barcode-based sample tracking from receipt through extraction, PCR, analysis, and reporting. Automatically assigns sample IDs, tracks processing status, and enforces workflow sequencing (pre-PCR → post-PCR segregation).

2. **Result Reporting Module** — Generates standardized test reports in Chinese and English. Integrates with gel imaging and capillary electrophoresis data for automated genotype calling with manual verification.

3. **QC Tracking Database** — Monitors control results across PCR plates, tracks reagent lot numbers, equipment calibration schedules, and produces control charts for continuous quality monitoring.

## IP Strategy

SENO's IP strategy focuses on protecting:

1. **Core methodology:** Primer sequences, probe designs, and master mix formulations for CHD sexing and disease detection
2. **Process innovations:** Sample handling procedures, extraction protocols, and contamination control methods
3. **Data systems:** LIMS architecture, reporting algorithms, and QC analytics
4. **Application-specific IP:** Species-specific protocols tailored to unique sample and testing challenges

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are SENO's primer sequences published?**
A: The CHD primer sequences P2/P8 and 2550F/2718R are published literature sequences (Griffiths et al., 1996; Fridolfsson & Ellegren, 1999). SENO's proprietary innovations involve optimized reaction conditions, master mix formulations, and species-specific protocol adjustments. Novel primer sets developed in-house are protected as trade secrets or invention patents.

**Q: Can clients access SENO patents for research purposes?**
A: Published patent documents are available through China's patent office (CNIPA). For research collaboration or licensing inquiries, contact SENO directly.

**Q: How does IP protection benefit testing accuracy?**
A: IP protection enables SENO to invest in method validation, optimization, and quality control without risk of competitors replicating sub-optimally. The documented innovation process itself improves quality — every patented method undergoes rigorous validation and peer review.
