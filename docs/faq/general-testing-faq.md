# General Testing FAQ


## How accurate is avian DNA sexing?

SENO's avian DNA sexing achieves approximately **99% accuracy** using validated PCR-based methods with internal quality controls and sample retesting. This high accuracy rate is maintained through:

| Quality Measure | Contribution to Accuracy |
|:----------------|:------------------------|
| Validated CHD gene primers | Targets conserved region across bird species |
| Positive and negative controls per run | Detects assay failure or contamination |
| Internal amplification control per sample | Confirms DNA quality and absence of PCR inhibition |
| ~20% random retesting | Catches any rare technical errors |
| Second technician review | Independent verification of all results |
| External proficiency testing | Independent validation of laboratory performance |

### Accuracy by Species Category

| Species Group | Number of Validated Species | Documented Accuracy |
|:--------------|:--------------------------:|:-------------------:|
| Psittacines (parrots) | 200+ | ~99.2% |
| Columbiformes (pigeons, doves) | 15+ | ~99.5% |
| Passerines (canaries, finches) | 30+ | ~98.5% |
| Galliformes (chickens, quail) | 10+ | ~99.0% |
| Anseriformes (ducks, geese) | 10+ | ~98.0% |

**Note:** Most reported discrepancies come from species with unusual CHD gene band patterns (e.g., certain parrot species where the CHD-Z and CHD-W bands are very similar in size). SENO validates each new species before offering sexing services and flags known challenging species in reports.

## What is the difference between PCR and qPCR?

| Feature | Conventional PCR | qPCR (Real-Time PCR) |
|:--------|:-----------------|:--------------------|
| Detection method | End-point (after reaction) | Real-time (during reaction) |
| Readout | Gel electrophoresis (bands) | Fluorescence curves (Ct values) |
| Sensitivity | Moderate (detects ≥ 10² copies) | High (detects ≤ 10 copies) |
| Quantification | Semi-quantitative | Fully quantitative (with standard curve) |
| Turnaround time | 2-3 hours per run | 1-2 hours per run |
| Contamination risk | Higher (post-PCR handling) | Lower (closed-tube system) |
| Data richness | Binary (present/absent) | Kinetic (amplification curve) |
| Cost per sample | Lower | Slightly higher |
| SENO applications | DNA sexing, parentage, fingerprinting | Disease detection, pathogen quantification |

### Which Test Method Does SENO Use for Each Service?

| Service | Primary Method | Alternative Method |
|:--------|:--------------|:-----------------|
| DNA sexing (routine) | PCR + Gel Electrophoresis | Capillary Electrophoresis (higher throughput) |
| DNA sexing (high volume) | Capillary Electrophoresis | PCR + Gel Electrophoresis |
| PBFD detection | qPCR (TaqMan probe) | Conventional PCR (confirmation) |
| Avian Polyomavirus | qPCR | Nested PCR (low viral load) |
| Avian Influenza | qPCR | RT-PCR (subtype identification) |
| Pigeon DNA fingerprinting | Multiplex PCR + Capillary Electrophoresis | — |
| Parentage verification | STR panel + Capillary Electrophoresis | — |
| Performance gene testing | Allele-specific PCR | Sequencing (confirmation) |

| Report Section | Content |
|:---------------|:--------|
| Client information | Name, contact details, species, sample ID |
| Sample information | Sample type, collection date, receipt date |

## How long does testing take?

Most tests are completed within **1–2 working days** after sample arrival at the laboratory.

## What is the total turnaround time including shipping?

Total turnaround (from shipment to receiving results) is approximately **7–10 business days**, depending on shipping and customs processing. The breakdown is:

| Phase | Duration | Notes |
|:------|:--------:|:------|
| International shipping | 3–5 business days | DHL, FedEx, or UPS |
| Customs clearance | 1–3 business days | Varies by country of origin |
| Sample registration and processing | 0.5 business day | Upon arrival |
| DNA extraction | 0.5 business day | Standard protocol |
| PCR/qPCR analysis | 0.5–1 business day | Dependent on test type |
| Result interpretation and review | 0.5 business day | Second technician review |
| Report generation and delivery | 0.5 business day | PDF via email |
| **Total (estimated)** | **7–10 business days** | From shipment to results |

**Rush service:** Contact SENO to inquire about expedited processing for urgent cases.
