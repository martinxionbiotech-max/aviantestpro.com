## Sample Collection

### What sample types are suitable for DNA testing?

| Sample Type | Best For | Collection Method | Key Requirement |
|:------------|:---------|:------------------|:----------------|
| Feathers (plucked) | DNA sexing, fingerprinting | Pluck 3–5 chest feathers | **Follicle must be visible** at base |
| Oral + cloacal swabs | Disease testing (qPCR) | Flocked swab, place in stabilization solution | Seal tube tightly |
| Blood (EDTA) | All tests | Venipuncture, 0.1–0.5 mL | Refrigerate, ship within 48 h |
| Blood (FTA card) | All tests | Spot blood on card, air dry | Dry completely before sealing |

### How do I collect and send feather samples for DNA sexing?

Pluck 3–5 freshly-growing feathers from the bird's chest or flank. Ensure the follicle bulb at the base is visible — this contains nucleated cells with DNA. **Critical: do not use shed feathers** — they lack viable follicular cells. Store feathers in a clean paper envelope (never plastic, which traps moisture and causes mold). Label each envelope with the bird's ID. Ship at room temperature within 4 weeks.

### How should I store samples before shipping?

- **Feathers:** Paper envelope, room temperature, dry location. No refrigeration.
- **Swabs (in stabilizer):** 2–8°C. Ship with cold pack if possible.
- **Blood (EDTA):** 2–8°C. Ship within 48 hours.
- **Blood (FTA card):** Air-dry completely, room temperature.
- **All types:** Ship within 4 weeks of collection for optimal DNA quality.

### Can I send samples from multiple birds together?

Yes, with proper separation. Each bird's feathers go in a separate paper envelope. Group envelopes in a single padded mailer. Place the submission form **outside** the bird envelopes (to prevent cross-contamination). Label each envelope clearly with the bird's unique ID.

### Can I pool samples from multiple birds to save cost?

Pooling is **not recommended for sexing** — mixed DNA produces ambiguous gender results. For disease surveillance, pooled testing (3–5 birds per pool) is available as a cost-saving option for flock-level screening.

| Pathogen | Affected Species | Assay Type | Notes |

| PiADV (Pigeon Adenovirus) | Pigeons | Multiplex qPCR | Part of 11-pathogen panel |
| PPMV-1 (Newcastle) | Pigeons, poultry | Multiplex qPCR | Part of 11-pathogen panel |

### How does the 11-pathogen Pigeon Disease Panel work?

One combined oral+cloacal swab sample is tested against 11 targets by multiplex qPCR. Each target has its own fluorescent probe channel plus an internal amplification control (VIC-labeled) to prevent false negatives from sample inhibition. Results are reported per target with Ct values.

### How do you interpret PBFD qPCR results?

| Ct Range | Interpretation | Action |
|:---------|:---------------|:-------|
| < 30 | Strong positive — active infection | Isolate, treat, re-test monthly |
| 30–35 | Moderate positive | Isolate, monitor closely |
| 35–38 | Weak positive — low viral load | Report "detected at low level"; retest in 2 weeks |
| 38–40 | Borderline | Suggest retest in 2 weeks |
| No Ct / > 40 | Negative | No action |

### Can PBFD-positive birds ever test negative later?

Yes. Some birds, particularly those with strong immune systems, can clear BFDV infection. Our case study data shows that 5/8 birds with initial low-positive results (Ct 32–38) tested negative after 8 weeks without intervention. Birds with Ct < 32 are less likely to clear the virus spontaneously.

## Racing Pigeon Genetics

### Can you detect performance-related genes in racing pigeons?

Yes. SENO tests four performance markers:

| Gene | Function | Association |
|:-----|:---------|:------------|
| DRD4 | Dopamine receptor | Homing motivation, race performance |
| LDHA | Lactate dehydrogenase | Anaerobic sprint capacity |
| CK | Creatine kinase | Energy metabolism |
| MB | Myoglobin | Oxygen transport in muscle |

Birds are categorized as Gold (top 12%), Silver (31%), Bronze (45%), or Base (12%). Based on 2,500 pigeons, Gold birds achieve top-10 finishes in 18% of races vs 3% for Base.

### Is performance gene testing a guarantee of race wins?

**No.** Genetic markers indicate potential, not destiny. Training, health, nutrition, weather conditions, and competition level all influence race outcomes. Performance testing is best used for **breeding decisions** — pairing birds with complementary genotypes to produce offspring with high genetic potential.

### How is DNA fingerprinting different from sexing?

| Aspect | DNA Sexing | DNA Fingerprinting |
|:-------|:-----------|:-------------------|
| Target | Single gene (CHD) | 12+ microsatellite markers |
| Purpose | Gender determination | Individual identification |
| Match probability | Not applicable | < 1 in 2.5 × 10¹⁵ |
| Use cases | Sex identification | Theft recovery, parentage, breeding records |
| Turnaround | 1–2 days | 3–5 days |

### Can you verify parentage in racing pigeons?

Yes. Using the same 12-marker panel, parentage is confirmed by comparing offspring alleles with putative parent profiles. For full trio verification (sire + dam + offspring), confidence exceeds 99.99%. Single-parent verification has lower but still highly reliable confidence. See [Parentage Verification](../pigeon-genetics/parentage-verification.md).

## Quality & Technology

### How does SENO ensure quality?

1. **Spatial separation:** Class 1,000 pre-PCR room + Class 10,000 post-PCR room, unidirectional workflow
2. **Controls in every batch:** Positive controls, negative controls, no-template controls, extraction blanks, internal amplification controls
3. **Filter tips only** — no aerosol contamination
4. **UV decontamination** before each work session
5. **External proficiency testing** annually through accredited providers
6. **All results reviewed by QC officer** before release

### What is the difference between PCR and qPCR?

| Feature | Conventional PCR | qPCR (Real-Time) |
|:--------|:----------------:|:-----------------:|
| Detection | Post-PCR gel electrophoresis | Real-time fluorescence |
| Quantification | No | Yes (Ct values → copy number) |
| Sensitivity | Moderate | Higher (10 copies/rxn LOD) |
| Turnaround | 3–4 hours | 1.5–2 hours |
| Best for | Sexing, genotyping | Disease detection, quantification |

### What certifications does SENO hold?

- **ISO 9001:2015** — Quality Management Systems
- **ISO 13485:2016** — Medical Devices Quality Management (for reagent manufacturing)
- **National High-Tech Enterprise** — China certification
- **CMA** — Testing laboratory qualification (China)

### How does SENO prevent cross-contamination between samples?

Each feather sample is handled with dedicated sterile forceps and scalpel blades. Pipetting uses filter tips exclusively. Pre- and post-PCR work is performed in physically separated rooms with dedicated equipment. NTC (no-template control) and extraction blanks are included in every batch. If NTC shows amplification, the entire batch is invalidated.
