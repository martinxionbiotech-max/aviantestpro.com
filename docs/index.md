### Documentation Categories

#### 🧬 DNA Gender Testing
Detailed technical references for avian sex determination using CHD-W/CHD-Z gene marker analysis:
- **PCR methodology** — P2/P8 and 2550F/2718R primer sets, hot-start Taq, 35-cycle protocol
- **Species compatibility** — Validated for 250+ species across Psittacidae, Columbidae, Fringillidae, Anatidae, Struthionidae, and more
- **Fragment analysis** — Capillary electrophoresis (preferred) vs. agarose gel electrophoresis
- **Interpretation** — Single band (male ZZ), two bands (female ZW), co-migration variants

#### 🔬 Avian Disease Testing
Molecular diagnostic assays for avian pathogens with published analytical validation:
- **PBFD (BFDV) qPCR** — TaqMan probe, ORF1 target, LOD 10 copies/reaction, 95.9% sensitivity, 98.0% specificity
- **Avian Polyomavirus (APV)** — PCR detection from feather, blood, or swab samples
- **Pigeon Disease Panel** — 11-pathogen multiplex qPCR including PiCV, PiHV, PiADV, Chlamydia psittaci, Salmonella, Candida, and PPMV-1
- **Avian Influenza Surveillance** — H5/H7/H9 subtyping RT-PCR for monitoring programs

#### 🕊️ Racing Pigeon Genetics
Advanced genetic analysis for racing pigeon breeding and identification:
- **Performance Gene Markers** — DRD4 (homing behavior), LDHA (energy metabolism), CK (sprint potential), MB (oxygen transport)
- **DNA Fingerprinting** — 12-locus microsatellite panel, >99.99% discrimination, <1 in 2.5×10¹⁵ match probability
- **Parentage Verification** — Exclusion-based parent confirmation with duo/trio analysis
- **Population Database** — 2,500+ pigeon profiles for allele frequency reference

#### 📋 Sample Collection & Shipping
Standardized protocols for proper sample handling across species:
- **Feather Collection** — Freshly plucked follicle preservation (3–7 feathers per bird)
- **Blood Sampling** — FTA card storage, EDTA tube, blood spot on filter paper
- **Swab Collection** — Oral and cloacal swab for disease detection, stabilization buffer
- **International Shipping** — IATA biological substance category B, CITES documentation, customs clearance
- **Storage & Stability** — Room temperature (feather), refrigeration (blood), stabilization buffer (swab)
