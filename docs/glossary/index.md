# Avian Genetics & DNA Testing — Glossary


## A — General Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Allele | — | One of two or more alternative forms of a gene, arising by mutation and found at the same place (locus) on a chromosome |
| Amplicon | — | DNA fragment produced by PCR amplification of a specific target sequence |
| Analytical Sensitivity | LOD | Limit of detection — the lowest concentration of target that can be reliably detected (≥ 95% of the time) |
| Analytical Specificity | — | Ability of an assay to detect only the intended target without cross-reacting with related or non-target organisms |
| Annealing | — | Step in the PCR cycle where primers bind (hybridize) to complementary sequences on the single-stranded DNA template |
| Avian Polyomavirus | APV | DNA virus (family Polyomaviridae) causing acute disease, particularly in young psittacines; characterized by hemorrhage, ascites, and sudden death |

## B — Diagnostic & Pathogen Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Beak and Feather Disease Virus | BFDV | Circovirus (family Circoviridae) that causes PBFD — progressive feather dystrophy, beak deformities, and immunosuppression in psittacines |
| Bland-Altman Analysis | — | Statistical method for comparing two measurement techniques by plotting the difference between paired measurements against their mean |
| Capillary Electrophoresis | CE | High-resolution DNA fragment separation method using a thin capillary filled with polymer matrix and fluorescence detection; used for microsatellite genotyping and STR analysis |

## C — CHD Gene & Sex Determination

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| CHD Gene | CHD | Chromodomain Helicase DNA-binding gene — a conserved gene family used as the standard target for avian DNA sexing because of its presence on both sex chromosomes with different intron sizes |
| CHD-W | — | Female-specific copy of the CHD gene located on the W chromosome (only present in ZW females) |
| CHD-Z | — | Copy of the CHD gene located on the Z chromosome (present in both ZZ males and ZW females) |
| Circovirus | — | Family Circoviridae — small (≈ 2 kb) circular single-stranded DNA viruses including BFDV, PiCV, and PCV2 |
| Clinical Sensitivity | DSe | Proportion of true positive samples correctly identified by the test — true positives / (true positives + false negatives) |
| Clinical Specificity | DSp | Proportion of true negative samples correctly identified by the test — true negatives / (true negatives + false positives) |
| Cloacal Swab | — | Sample collection method using a sterile swab inserted approximately 1 cm into the cloaca and rotated gently to collect epithelial cells and microorganisms |
| CMA | — | China Metrology Accreditation — Chinese equivalent of ISO/IEC 17025 for laboratory competence |
| CNAS | — | China National Accreditation Service — the national accreditation body for conformity assessment in China |
| Ct Value | Ct | Cycle threshold — in qPCR, the PCR cycle number at which the fluorescence signal of the target crosses the background threshold; inversely proportional to the initial target quantity |
| Denaturation | — | Step in the PCR cycle where double-stranded DNA is heated (typically 94–98°C) to separate it into single strands |
| Diagnostic Sensitivity | DSe | Same as clinical sensitivity — see above |
| Diploid | — | Having two complete sets of chromosomes (2n) — all somatic cells in birds are diploid |

## D — DNA & Genetic Profiling

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Discrimination Power | DP | In DNA fingerprinting: the probability that two randomly selected individuals have different genotypes; DP > 0.9999 is considered acceptable for forensic identification |
| DNA Fingerprinting | — | Genetic profiling technique using microsatellite (STR) markers to generate a unique DNA profile for individual identification, parentage verification, and population studies |
| DNA Sexing | — | Molecular determination of biological sex by detecting the presence or absence of sex chromosome-specific DNA markers (CHD-W for females, CHD-Z for both sexes) |
| DRD4 | — | Dopamine receptor D4 gene — encodes a G-protein-coupled receptor involved in dopamine signaling; polymorphisms associated with exploratory behaviour, homing motivation, and stress tolerance in racing pigeons |
| Dynamic Range | — | In quantitative assays: the range of target concentrations over which the assay produces a proportional signal, bounded by LOD and the highest quantifiable concentration |

## E — Extraction & Sample Terminology

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| EDTA | — | Ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid — a chelating agent used as an anticoagulant in blood collection tubes (purple-top tubes); binds calcium, preventing blood clotting |
| Electropherogram | — | Graphical output of capillary electrophoresis showing a series of peaks, each representing detected DNA fragments (by size and fluorescence intensity) |
| Extension | — | Step in the PCR cycle where Taq polymerase adds nucleotides to the primer, extending the new DNA strand (typically at 72°C) |
| FTA Card | — | Specialized filter paper impregnated with chemicals that lyse cells, denature proteins, and stabilize nucleic acids at room temperature for extended periods |

## G — Genetics & Genomics Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Genotype | — | The genetic constitution of an individual at a specific locus or set of loci — usually represented as a pair of alleles (e.g., AA, Aa, aa) |
| Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium | HWE | Principle stating that allele and genotype frequencies in a population remain constant from generation to generation in the absence of evolutionary influences; deviation may indicate selection, inbreeding, or population structure |
| Heterogametic | — | Having two different sex chromosomes — in birds, females are heterogametic (ZW) |
| Heterozygosity | — | The proportion of individuals in a population that carry two different alleles at a given locus; a measure of genetic diversity |
| Homogametic | — | Having two identical sex chromosomes — in birds, males are homogametic (ZZ) |

## I — ISO & Quality Standards

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| ISO 13485 | — | International standard for quality management systems specific to medical devices, including in vitro diagnostic (IVD) products |
| ISO 9001 | — | International standard for general quality management systems, applicable to any organization |
| LDHA | — | Lactate dehydrogenase A gene — encodes the A subunit of lactate dehydrogenase; catalyzes the conversion of pyruvate to lactate in anaerobic metabolism; associated with sprint performance and recovery in racing pigeons |
| Levey-Jennings Chart | — | Quality control chart used to monitor assay performance over time by plotting measured values (e.g., Ct of a control sample) against established mean and standard deviation limits |

## M — Microsatellite & Molecular Markers

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Microsatellite | STR | Short Tandem Repeat — a DNA sequence motif of 2–6 base pairs repeated in tandem; highly polymorphic and widely used for DNA fingerprinting, parentage analysis, and population genetics |
| Multiplex PCR | — | Simultaneous amplification of two or more target sequences in a single PCR reaction using multiple primer pairs; used extensively in SENO's 11-pathogen disease panel |
| NMPA | — | National Medical Products Administration (China) — the regulatory authority overseeing medical devices, IVD reagents, and pharmaceutical products in China |
| Nucleic Acid | — | DNA (deoxyribonucleic acid) or RNA (ribonucleic acid) — the genetic material of all living organisms |

## P — Pathogen & Disease Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Pathogen | — | Microorganism capable of causing disease — includes viruses, bacteria, and fungi |
| PCR | Polymerase Chain Reaction | Method to exponentially amplify a specific DNA sequence through repeated cycles of denaturation, annealing, and extension using a thermostable DNA polymerase |
| PiADV | — | Pigeon Adenovirus — DNA virus causing hepatic necrosis and enteritis ("young bird sickness") in pigeons |
| PiCV | — | Pigeon Circovirus — small circular ssDNA virus causing immunosuppression in young pigeons, similar to BFDV in psittacines |
| PiHV | — | Pigeon Herpes Virus — DNA virus causing respiratory distress and hepatic necrosis in pigeons |
| Polymorphism | — | Naturally occurring genetic variation in a population — may be a single nucleotide change (SNP), repeat length variation (VNTR), or structural variant |
| PPMV-1 | — | Pigeon Paramyxovirus Type 1 — RNA virus closely related to Newcastle Disease Virus, causing neurological signs, green diarrhea, and mortality in pigeons |
| Primer | — | Short (18–25 nucleotide) single-stranded DNA sequence designed to bind specifically to a complementary region on the target DNA; provides the 3' hydroxyl group for Taq polymerase to begin extension |
| Probability of Identity | PI | In DNA fingerprinting: the probability that two individuals share the same genotype by chance; PI > 10⁶ is considered acceptable for individual identification |
| Proficiency Testing | PT | External quality assessment where a laboratory's test results are compared against a reference laboratory or peer group to ensure accuracy |
| Psittacine | — | Bird belonging to the order Psittaciformes — the parrot family, including macaws, cockatoos, African greys, Amazons, parakeets, and lovebirds |

## Q — Quantitative PCR & Assay Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| qPCR | Quantitative PCR | Real-time PCR — amplifies and simultaneously quantifies target DNA by measuring fluorescence emitted during each cycle; uses either double-stranded DNA-binding dyes (SYBR Green) or target-specific probes (TaqMan) |
| ROC Curve | ROC | Receiver Operating Characteristic curve — plots true positive rate (sensitivity) against false positive rate (1 − specificity) across all possible threshold values; AUC ≥ 0.95 indicates excellent discrimination |
| RT-PCR | Reverse Transcription PCR | PCR preceded by conversion of RNA to complementary DNA (cDNA) using reverse transcriptase; required for detection of RNA viruses such as PPMV-1, NDV, and AIV |

## S — Sample & Species Terms

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| Sensitivity | — | Refers to analytical sensitivity (LOD) or clinical sensitivity (DSe) depending on context — see specific entries above |
| Specificity | — | Refers to analytical specificity or clinical specificity (DSp) depending on context — see specific entries above |
| STR | Short Tandem Repeat | See microsatellite above |
| Taq Polymerase | — | Heat-stable DNA polymerase originally isolated from *Thermus aquaticus*; used in PCR for its ability to withstand the high temperatures required for DNA denaturation |

## Z — Sex Chromosome System

| Term | Abbreviation | Definition |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------|
| ZW System | — | Avian sex chromosome system: males are homogametic (ZZ), females are heterogametic (ZW). This is opposite to the mammalian XY system where males (XY) are heterogametic |
